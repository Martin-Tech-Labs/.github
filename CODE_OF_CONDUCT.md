# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to participate in a respectful, welcoming community.
Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to treat each other with dignity,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and work, rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Respect people's privacy, boundaries, and time.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and adjust your
  behavior when it causes harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualized content directed
  at community members.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information without
  their permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in resolving it.

## Scope

This policy applies to the organization's repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other community spaces. It also applies when someone represents
this community publicly.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer or organization owner using
an available private contact method listed on their GitHub profile or the
organization's website. Do not post sensitive details or personal information in
public issues or discussions.

Include relevant links, a description of what happened, and any context that
helps explain the concern. If the report concerns a maintainer, contact another
maintainer or organization owner where possible.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share information only as needed
to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will assess reports fairly, consider the circumstances and impact,
and give those involved an opportunity to provide context where appropriate.

Depending on severity and whether behavior is repeated, responses may include:

- A request to change behavior or a private warning.
- Editing or removing inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- A permanent ban from community spaces.

Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Maintainers are subject to
this policy as well.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request a review privately,
providing relevant context. Where possible, a maintainer who was not involved in
the original decision will review the request.
